About tech fest

Welcome to TECH FEST 2026, a celebration of technology, creativity, innovation, and limitless possibilities, hosted by SSIT × upGrad School of Technology (UGSOT) on 26 November 2026. Whether you're passionate about Artificial Intelligence, Coding, Robotics, or the ever-evolving world of technology, this is your opportunity to learn, experiment, collaborate, and create something extraordinary. Join us for a day filled with innovation, energy, creativity, and unforgettable experiences.
